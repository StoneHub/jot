# Jot 0.2.6

A dictation shortcut press that cannot start now says why in the notice and in jot status, for example that the microphone is off. After wake or an input change, a microphone that fails to start is retried for about half a minute before Jot reports the error. Resume restarts the microphone when the models are already loaded, and the menu bar shows Resume with "Microphone off" in that state.
